# Privacy

MaevePaint collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Pictures, settings and downloaded sets stay on your computer. Pictures go in the `MaevePaint`
folder inside your Pictures folder. Everything else goes in `%APPDATA%\MaevePaint\data` on
Windows and `~/.local/share/maevepaint` on Linux.

The app goes online only when a grown-up opens **Content > Get more** in the grown-ups' window.
It reads the list of sets from this repo's `library` release on GitHub, then downloads the sets
the grown-up installs from the same place. GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any
download, and its
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
covers what it does with it. The app sends nothing about you or your pictures.
