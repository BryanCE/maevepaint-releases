# Privacy

MaevePaint collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Pictures, settings and downloaded sets stay on your computer: pictures in the `MaevePaint`
folder in your Pictures folder, everything else in `%APPDATA%\MaevePaint\data`.

The app only goes online when a grown-up opens **Content > Get more** in the grown-ups' window.
It then reads the list of sets from this repository's `library` release on GitHub, and downloads
the sets the grown-up installs from there. Those requests go to GitHub, which sees your IP
address as it does for any download; see
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Nothing about you or your pictures is sent.
